Frequently asked

The widget shows zeros but the app shows hours.

Remove the widget and add it back. iOS rate-limits how often widgets refresh, so a freshly installed widget can lag behind the app by a few minutes.

I forgot to press Start. Can I still log the drive?

Yes. Open the Log tab and tap + to enter the drive by hand — set the start time, end time, and whether it was a night drive.

I pressed Stop an hour late.

Tap the drive in the Log tab and correct the end time. The totals recalculate immediately. Drives over four hours also show a warning when you stop them, as a prompt to check.

How does the app decide a drive was at night?

By the clock only. A drive starting after your night cutoff — 8:00 PM by default, changeable in Settings — or before 5:00 AM is suggested as a night drive. You confirm or correct the flag before it saves. The app does not use your location, the motion sensors, or sunset data.

Does a drive that crosses midnight count twice?

No. It stays one drive, filed under the day it began, and the Log shows “(next day)” next to the end time.

What happens if the app closes mid-drive?

Nothing is lost. The timer is computed from the saved start instant rather than a running counter, so force-quitting the app, restarting the phone, or letting the battery die all leave the elapsed time correct when you reopen it.

How do I get the log to the DMV?

Tap Share Log on the Log tab. You get a formatted text file you can print and a CSV you can open in a spreadsheet, both with totals already summed.

Where is my data stored? Can I get it back if I delete the app?

Everything is stored on your device only. There is no account and no cloud backup, so deleting the app deletes the log. Export it with Share Log before you do.

Can I track more than one teen?

Yes. Add a second driver in Settings. Each driver keeps their own drives, their own hour goals, and their own exported log.

Please don't use this app while driving. Practice Hours is built for the supervising adult in the passenger seat. Start and stop the timer while the car is parked. The person behind the wheel should never handle the phone.

About the hour requirements

Practice Hours is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by any DMV, state agency, or licensing authority. Supervised-driving requirements vary by state and change over time — check your own state's rules for the number of hours you need and the form they expect.
