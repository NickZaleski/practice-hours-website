Privacy Policy

Practice Hours for iPhone · Effective 11 October 2026

Your driving log never leaves your device.

Every drive, time, note, and driver name you enter is stored on your iPhone and is never uploaded. There is no account and no sign-up. The only information that leaves the app is anonymous subscription data, described below.

What stays on your device, always

The practice log itself stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone, inside a container shared only between Practice Hours and its own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets:

WhatWhy
Driver names you typeTo label the log and its exported header
Drive start and end timesTo calculate duration and totals
Day / night flag and optional notesTo break totals down and let you annotate a drive
Your hour goals and night cutoffTo draw progress and suggest the night flag

None of this is transmitted anywhere. I have no server for it, and I never see it.

What is sent, and to whom

Practice Hours uses Adapty to manage subscriptions and purchases. Adapty acts as my data processor. When the app launches it sends:

DataPurpose
A device identifier (Apple's vendor identifier) and basic device details such as iOS version, model, country, and languageTo recognise the same install across launches so a purchase can be restored
Purchase and subscription history — what was bought, when, renewal and trial statusTo unlock paid features, restore purchases, and report revenue
Product interaction — which paywall was shown, and whether it was dismissed or convertedAnalytics, to understand whether the paid features are worth offering

This data is pseudonymous: it is tied to a randomly generated profile identifier for your installation, not to your name, email, or Apple ID. I do not set a user identifier, so it is not linked to your identity.

Your driving log is never part of this. Drives, times, notes, and driver names are not sent to Adapty or anywhere else.

What the app does not do

  • No account, login, email address, or phone number.
  • No location, GPS, motion sensors, microphone, camera, contacts, or photos.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with data brokers. The app does not ask for tracking permission because it does not track.
  • No uploading of your practice log, under any circumstances.
  • No cloud sync or backup managed by this app.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. I never see your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Exporting your log

Tapping Share Log builds a text file and a CSV file on your device and hands them to the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where they go — Mail, Files, Messages, AirDrop, a printer. The app does not upload them, keep a copy, or receive any information about where you sent them. Once a file leaves the app, it is governed by whatever service you chose to send it to.

Children

Practice Hours is designed for a supervising adult, not for children, and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups turned on in iOS, your Practice Hours log may be included in those backups the same way any app's data is. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Delete an individual drive by swiping it in the Log tab, or delete a driver in Settings to remove them and their drives. Deleting the app removes everything it stored locally — export your log first if you still need it, because it cannot be recovered afterwards.

To have the subscription data associated with your installation deleted, email me at sidfender27@gmail.com and I will request its removal from Adapty.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the data described above, and to object to its processing. Because the practice log never leaves your device, you already hold it directly. For the subscription data, write to the address above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects anything beyond what is described here, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
