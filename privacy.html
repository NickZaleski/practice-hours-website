Privacy Policy

Practice Hours for iPhone · Effective 11 October 2026

Practice Hours does not collect any data.

There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party code of any kind. The app makes no network requests. Nothing you enter leaves your device unless you deliberately export it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app records stays in its own storage on your iPhone, inside a container shared only between Practice Hours and its own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets:

WhatWhy
Driver names you typeTo label the log and its exported header
Drive start and end timesTo calculate duration and totals
Day / night flag and optional notesTo break totals down and let you annotate a drive
Your hour goals and night cutoffTo draw progress and suggest the night flag

None of this is transmitted anywhere. I have no server, and I never see it.

What the app does not do

  • No account, login, email address, or phone number.
  • No location, GPS, motion sensors, microphone, camera, contacts, or photos.
  • No analytics, telemetry, attribution, or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs or frameworks. The app has no dependencies.
  • No cloud sync or backup managed by this app.

Exporting your log

Tapping Share Log builds a text file and a CSV file on your device and hands them to the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where they go — Mail, Files, Messages, AirDrop, a printer. The app does not upload them, keep a copy, or receive any information about where you sent them. Once a file leaves the app, it is governed by whatever service you chose to send it to.

Children

Practice Hours is designed for a supervising adult, not for children, and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups turned on in iOS, your Practice Hours log may be included in those backups the same way any app's data is. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Delete an individual drive by swiping it in the Log tab, or delete a driver in Settings to remove them and their drives. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is nothing held anywhere else, so there is no deletion request to send me — but export your log first if you still need it, because it cannot be recovered afterwards.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
